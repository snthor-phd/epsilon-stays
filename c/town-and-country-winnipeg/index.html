Town & Country Campground Epsilon Approved

Winnipeg, MB, Canada · Private

Big private campground just east of Winnipeg, and the rendezvous point for the Northern Lights & Polar Bears caravan. Level sites, full hookups, and pull-throughs long enough to take anything we'd bring. Sites sit a little close together, but there's still plenty of room to live outside. Laundry is clean and well kept - $2.75 CAD to wash, $2.00 to dry. There's a store on site with fresh ice cream, and a restaurant area. We took site 165, and most of the rest look just as good.
