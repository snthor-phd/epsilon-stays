Camp Grayling Trailer Park

Grayling, MI, USA · Other

Michigan National Guard trailer park inside the gate at Camp Grayling. About 70 wooded sites, 20/30-amp with water, sewer and cable; only five 50-amp big-rig sites. Eligibility limited to military, retired, DAV, veteran caregivers and DoD civilians — bring the credential to the gate. Season runs first Saturday in May through September 30.
