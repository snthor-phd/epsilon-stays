Juniper Campground (TRNP North Unit) Epsilon Approved

Watford City, ND, USA · National Park

Dry camping in the quieter North Unit, five miles in from the Highway 85 entrance along the Little Missouri. No hookups anywhere in the campground, generators 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. local. Wildlife comes through the campground rather than the other way around.
