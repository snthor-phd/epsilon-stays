Wild State Cider

Duluth, MN, USA · Harvest Hosts

A working cidery and taproom in Duluth, not a campground — an overnight stop through Harvest Hosts. Great people. Good for small rigs only, under 30 feet, and room for only one rig as far as we could see. The pizza is above average and the cider is very good. A nice place to meet friends. Harvest Hosts etiquette applies: arrive within business hours, one night, and buy something.
